Appendix: Methodology, Ruleset & Known Limitations

Ruleset Version: {{ custody.ruleset_version }} • Engine: PECFF v{{ custody.engine_version }}

Methodology: Passive TCP flow reassembly, deterministic protocol parsing, NIST SP 800-57 pure deterministic risk engine, and unsupervised 94-dimensional IsolationForest outlier detection.

Known Forensic Limitations:

  • TLS 1.3 Certificate Opacity: In TLS 1.3 handshakes, server certificate messages are encrypted under handshake traffic keys and cannot be observed passively without out-of-band TLS key logs (NSS KeyLog).
  • Encrypted Client Hello (ECH): In flows utilizing ECH, SNI and client parameters are encrypted against public Outer SNI DNS records.
  • Session Resumption (TLS Tickets): Resumed handshakes omit full certificate exchange and key negotiation phases.
  • Passive-Only Revocation: Certificate revocation is evaluated against cached offline CRL/OCSP records and CRLSets; real-time OCSP responder queries are disabled to maintain passive airgap safety.
